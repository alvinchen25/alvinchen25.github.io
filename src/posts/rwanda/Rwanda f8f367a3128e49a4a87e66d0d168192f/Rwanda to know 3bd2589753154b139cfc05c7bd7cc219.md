Greetings to know!

Muraho - respectful, to elders, haven’t seen somebody in a while

Bite - Informal, B sort of silent

mwaramutse - good morning

Mwiriwe - good afternoon, more daily use

amakuru - how are you

Nitwa ___, e.g. Nitwa Alvin

# January 23, 2025

It's one of our last few days of teaching. At this point we are mostly doing review, so we do a geo and a combo review.

Serena and Zian have both been staying overnight with the students. We all sort of want to hang out with the kids after class, but Namgay isn't really willing to drive us late. Unlike our first driver, Sangay, Namgay isn't employed by the Ministry and he isn't really friends with Dorji. He was sort of just assigned to us when Sangay left, but of course we don't want to make him work crazy hours or anything. One time we stayed for a bit after and he seemed a bit unhappy, which is reasonable, since I'm sure he has other places he wants to be.

So as a result, Serena and Zian have been staying over, but not both at the same time, which is nice because it means I always have someone to talk to for dinner and breakfast.

I decide to not stay with the students, because my sleep isn't really recovered, and I've been spending my evenings calling friends or doing writing or other things like that. This has been a wonderful experience but I do miss home a bit and being in a college-like environment with a bunch of friends. It also would potentially be more awkward for me, because I think in some ways I've been balancing things out a bit, since Zian mostly hangs out with the boys and Serena with the girls. I've been trying to talk to everyone. But mostly I don't stay over because it would be awkward for me to wake up at 4 am if I were living in their room.

We do some AMC style combinatorics in the morning, and I think it's a good way to practice reading comprehension for math problems and also get used to using basic counting techniques.

The coolest part of teaching today was the Countdown Round. Serena had the problems from previous teaching experiences, and she made a bracket and had students come up and compete with each other mathcounts style. I think the kids had a lot of fun with it.

I've been going on a lot more walks and runs, just appreciating what it's like outside. Apparently it's been a dry season, and on one of the drives home we see smoke coming out from behind a mountain. Namgay tells us there's been a lot of forest fires, and apparently in other areas of the country there are many more. We are in a lower, warmer area, which has generally more moisture.
